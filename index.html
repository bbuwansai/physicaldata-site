    Different by design

    Four things nobody else records

    Hours, not minutes.

    Whole jobs from start to finish, one to four hours, no cuts. Enough context for a model to plan and remember across a shift, not a clip.

    1 h 2 h 3 h 30 s typical demonstration 15 min longest published robot memory 3 h 14 min one Physical Data session
    Drawn to scale on a 4 hour line

    Mistakes, kept.

    Perfect demonstrations show success, not the way back to it. Every drop, spill and wrong turn stays in the data, with the recovery labeled, so a model learns to notice and fix, not only to repeat.

    Reasoning, out loud.

    Collectors say what they plan, decide and remember as they work, and when they change the plan, and why. Timestamped to the frame, so the thought and the action can be learned together.

    Same place, many times.

    The same job in the same home across days, clutter, light and people, and across many homes. A model learns what can happen in a place, not one lucky run.
